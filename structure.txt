1. Features
Add new recipes.
View all saved recipes.
Search recipes by name or ingredient.
Filter recipes by category.
Update recipe details.
Delete recipes.
Store ingredients and preparation instructions.
Track preparation and cooking time.
Generate a recipe summary report.
